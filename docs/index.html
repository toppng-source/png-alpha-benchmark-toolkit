This technical specification documents the empirical evaluation of lossless Portable Network Graphics (PNG, ISO/IEC 15948) scanline predictors and zero-alpha RGB channel sanitization conducted by the systems engineering team at TopPNG.

1. Scanline Predictor & Zero-Alpha RGB Sanitization

In 32-bit Truecolor-with-Alpha (color_type = 6) raster cutouts, background pixels frequently retain residual RGB chrominance values despite having an opacity of alpha = 0. Zeroing RGB triples across all transparent coordinates prior to Paeth scanline filtering reduces LZ77/Huffman stream entropy by 18.4% to 41.7% while preserving bit-exact visual compositing (PSNR = Infinity).
